What we need from you

Already decided: no donations, no ads, no tracking, free to run. Three questions remain.

Which English translation should be the default?

Every edition has its own wording and its own license. The name of the edition is shown in the app, so the choice should be yours.

Who will review the reminder pool and spot-check each release?

A qualified person must confirm that each reminder stands on its own and that a sample matches a trusted printed copy. This is the most important role in the project.

A free address at launch, or a custom domain?

The free pages.dev address costs nothing. A custom domain is the only possible cost and is entirely optional.
